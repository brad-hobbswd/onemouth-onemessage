CHAPTER 4

The Words That Waited Years to Be Healed

Some sentences leave echoes that remain for years.

"Therewith bless we God... and therewith curse we men..." James 3:9–10

Grace remembered the sentence perfectly.

"You're too sensitive."

Daniel spoke those words years earlier with a smile that carried dismissal rather than understanding. Time passed, birthdays came and went, holidays gathered everyone around tables, yet the words remained.

Conversations stayed polite. Distance hid beneath routine family interactions. Silence became easier than honesty.

Then Thanksgiving arrived again.

Lila noticed everything. Small expressions. Forced smiles. Familiar tension. She sensed old wounds living beneath ordinary moments.
