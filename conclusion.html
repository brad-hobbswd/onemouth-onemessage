One Mouth, One Witness

Every word leaves something behind.

Words rarely disappear after being spoken. Conversations continue echoing through hearts, homes, churches, friendships, and memories. A sentence spoken in anger can remain for years. A word filled with grace can become healing someone never forgets.

Every chapter in this journey revealed one truth. Speech carries influence. Words build or tear down. They strengthen or wound. They invite peace or deepen division.

"The tongue cannot be tamed, but it can be surrendered."

Human effort alone cannot master the tongue. Transformation begins through surrender to Jesus. A surrendered voice becomes more than communication. A surrendered voice becomes testimony.

Every conversation becomes an opportunity to bring life. Every sentence becomes a choice. Every mouth becomes a witness.

One mouth. One message. One witness.
