CHAPTER 7

The Record That Cannot Be Edited

Words become part of the legacy left behind.

"If a man say, I love God, and hateth his brother, he is a liar..." 1 John 4:20

Benjamin had lived long enough to understand that words mattered.

Years carried memories. Conversations remained. Faces changed. Seasons moved forward. Spoken words continued leaving traces behind.

Retirement brought people together to celebrate his life and years of service.

Stories began filling the room.

"You spoke hope when I had none." "You corrected me without humiliating me." "You always spoke with kindness."

Benjamin listened quietly. Gratitude filled his heart.
