CHAPTER 6

Blessing the One Who Spoke Against You

Grace often carries greater power than retaliation.

"Bless them that curse you." Matthew 5:44

Carla replayed the conversation repeatedly in her mind.

The words felt sharp. Public. Unnecessary.

"I don't trust her." "She's fake."

Those sentences landed harder than Carla expected. Service, kindness, generosity, and faithfulness suddenly felt invisible beneath criticism.

Anger followed her home. Frustration filled her thoughts. Explanations formed naturally. Defenses seemed ready.

Yet another thought surfaced. Bless them that curse you.
