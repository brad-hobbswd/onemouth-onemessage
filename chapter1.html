CHAPTER 1

The Hidden Poison of Words

Words carry unseen influence and lasting impact.

"But the tongue can no man tame; it is an unruly evil, full of deadly poison." James 3:8

Elena woke before sunrise, as she did every morning. The quiet streets of Brightwood reflected her own inner stillness. Today, though, there was a heaviness she could not shake.

She had spent the previous week overhearing whispers in the teacher's lounge. Subtle comments about a student named James continued surfacing in her mind. Words spoken carelessly often leave marks unseen by others.

Walking into her classroom, she greeted James with warmth and kindness. Something inside her already sensed that a deeper battle had begun.
