CHAPTER 5

The Sanctuary Where Words Still Wounded

Some wounds come from voices that once felt safest.

"Therewith bless we God, even the Father; and therewith curse we men... these things ought not so to be." James 3:9–10

Church had always felt safe to Janelle, until something shifted.

Third row. Same notebook. Same Bible resting on her lap every Sunday. Worship still moved her heart. Prayer still mattered deeply. Yet a heaviness quietly settled over her spirit.

Small comments began surfacing around hallways and conversations.

"Did you hear about the worship leader?" "We should pray about leadership."

The words sounded spiritual, yet something beneath them felt different. Concern slowly became speculation. Prayer requests carried details that felt heavier than wisdom.
